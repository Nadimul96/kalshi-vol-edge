Why this matters

Kalshi's BTC markets settle on the BRTI: the simple average of sixty one-second readings of an index built from Coinbase, Bitstamp, Kraken, Gemini and LMAX Digital. No single exchange is the settlement price.

This package prices off Coinbase, a constituent, and measures the gap rather than assuming it away. When the model's lead over the strike is smaller than the basis, the outcome is decided by index composition, not by volatility.
